`RangeScale` shows where one value sits between a low end and a high end: a resting heart rate between 40 and 120, a body mass index, a price between its day's low and high. The marker sits at the value, the ends are written under the track, and `.show_value()` writes the value above the marker.

```rust
use navree::ui::charts::*;
use navree::ui::*;

RangeScale::new("Resting heart rate", 58)
    .range(40, 120)
    .format(NumberFormat::number().suffix(" bpm"))
    .show_value()
```

## The track

The track is a quiet bar. `.track(Gradient)` paints a gradient over it, through any stops you give, along the scale from its low end (offset 0) to its high end (1). Use a horizontal gradient and the opacity of a stop to soften it. Stops take theme colors, so the track is right in light and dark.

```rust
use navree::ui::charts::*;
use navree::ui::*;

RangeScale::new("Resting heart rate", 58)
    .range(40, 120)
    .track(
        Gradient::horizontal()
            .stop_with_opacity(0.0, Ink::tone(Tone::Success), 0.55)
            .stop_with_opacity(0.3, Ink::tone(Tone::named("cyan")), 0.55)
            .stop_with_opacity(0.6, Ink::tone(Tone::named("orange")), 0.55)
            .stop_with_opacity(1.0, Ink::tone(Tone::Danger), 0.55),
    )
```

Colour is never the only way to read the scale: the end labels, the summary and the table say the numbers.

## Ends and values

The ends are written in the chart's number format and locale (`.locale("en-IN")` writes `₹10,00,000`). `.ends("Underweight", "Obese")` writes words under the ends instead; the summary and the table still give the numbers.

A value outside the scale sits at the nearer end, and the summary says it is below or above. Reversed ends are put in order, and equal ends make a scale one wide.

## Motion

`.motion(Motion::new().delay(300))` pops the marker in. It is drawn where it ends, so a reader who asks for less motion sees it in place.

## States

A scale with no value is empty, and the states work as on a [line chart](/docs/components/line-chart/#loading-failed-and-empty).

## The table

One row: the value and the two ends. Its table is for screen readers only by default, as there is nothing to switch. `.table(TableMode::Switch)` brings the switch back.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- The scale is a picture named by its title and described by its summary: "58 bpm on a scale from 40 bpm to 120 bpm." It is not a tab stop, because it has one value.
- The drawing and the end labels under it are hidden from assistive technology: the summary and the table say the same.
- The marker is drawn in the page's ink with a ring of the page's color, so it reads on every part of the track in light and dark.

</UiA11y>

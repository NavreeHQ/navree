`Card` is a surface with 6 px corners. Its header holds a title, a description and an action; its body and footer hold anything. Each part is drawn only when you fill it.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Card::titled("Sutlej Supply renewal")
    .description("Annual platform license, 120 seats.")
    .body(Text::new("Procurement asked for a two-year term."))
    .footer(Button::primary("Mark won"))
```

## When to use it

Use a card to set related content apart: a summary, a chart, a form. For a list of things of the same kind, use [Items](/docs/components/item/) or a [Table](/docs/components/table/).

## Variants

`.variant(CardVariant)` changes how the card is drawn. The default is a line around a surface; `Muted` is a filled surface with no line, for a quiet group; `Ghost` has no line and no fill, only the card's spacing, for content that stands alone; `Dashed` has a dashed line, for a place to add something.

```rust
Card::new()
    .variant(CardVariant::Dashed)
    .body(Text::muted("No saved views yet."))
    .footer(Button::outline("Add a view"))
```

## Raised

`.raised()` puts the theme's soft shadow under the card, the one a theme with `Elevation::Soft` gives a raised part; a flat theme draws none, so the card is drawn as before. It links the looks stylesheet.

```rust
Card::titled("Plan").raised().body(Text::new("₹2,990 a year"))
```

## On the inverse surface

`CardVariant::Inverse` draws the card on the theme's other palette: a dark card on a light page, a light one on a dark page. Everything in it reads the flipped colours with no work of its own: its title and text, a badge, a primary button (which takes the surface's text colour, so a white button with dark text on a near-black card) and an outline button.

```rust
Card::titled("Security, in every mode")
    .variant(CardVariant::Inverse)
    .body(Text::new("Every team works in its own pipeline."))
    .footer(Button::primary("Request a quote"))
```

<UiPreview example="card/inverse" />

With no colours of its own, the inverse surface is the theme's other palette, which every theme already passes for contrast. A theme of your own sets its surface, raised panels, lines, text and muted text with `Roles::inverse` (see [Themes](/docs/ui/themes/)), and `Theme::with_roles` refuses any of them whose text falls short of WCAG AA, in light and in dark. The card is marked `data-inverse` and links no stylesheet of its own.

## Headings

The title is an `<h2>`, which suits a card under a page's `<h1>`. Under a section's heading, `.heading(3)` makes it an `<h3>`, and so on, so the outline of the page stays in order.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- A card is a `<div>`: it isn't focusable and has no role. What's inside it keeps its own.
- Its title is a real heading, so screen reader users can jump between cards.

</UiA11y>

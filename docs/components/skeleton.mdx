`Skeleton` holds the place of content that is coming, in its shape, so the page doesn't jump when it arrives. A lazy [Region](/docs/ui/regions/) shows two skeleton lines until its content loads.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Skeleton::new(SkeletonShape::Line)
```

## When to use it

Use it when you know the shape of what is coming.

<UiChoose name="loading" current="Skeleton" />

## In a page

A report that takes a moment to compute does not hold up the rest of the page. Its region loads after the page has painted and shows skeleton lines until its content arrives:

```rust
Card::titled("Revenue by month").body(
    Region::new("revenue").src("/fragments/revenue").lazy(),
)
```

The page does not jump when the report arrives, because the lines hold its place. [Regions and live updates](/docs/ui/regions/) covers the source, polling and failures.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- A skeleton has `aria-hidden="true"`: it says nothing. Mark what is loading as busy instead; a lazy region sets `aria-busy` for you.
- The pulse stops when the reader asks for reduced motion.

</UiA11y>

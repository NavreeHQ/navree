`Waterfall` shows how an amount becomes another, a step at a time: what customers paid down to what reached the bank, revenue down to profit. Each step's bar starts where the last one ended, so it floats at the running total. A step that adds runs on in one colour, a step that takes away runs back in another, and a total is a bar from zero in a third. Each row writes its amount at its end, a step's with its sign.

```rust
use navree::ui::charts::*;
use navree::ui::*;

Waterfall::new("This month's settlement")
    .description("From what the store's customers paid to what reached the bank")
    .locale("en-IN")
    .format(NumberFormat::currency("₹").max_decimals(0))
    .start("Orders", 2_50_000)
    .step("Store fee", -25_000)
    .step("Tax on the fee", -4_500)
    .step("Returns", -12_000)
    .step("Promotions refunded", 3_000)
    .step("Tax withheld", -2_500)
    .total("Paid to the bank")
```

`.start(label, amount)` is a bar from zero, drawn as a total, where the running total begins. `.step(label, amount)` adds to the running total when the amount is above zero and takes away when it is below. `.total(label)` draws a bar from zero to the running total so far: a subtotal between steps, or the net at the end. The rows are drawn in the order you add them.

## When to use it

Use a waterfall when the reader needs to see what each step did to a total: fees and taxes off a settlement, costs off revenue. To compare amounts that don't add up to one another, use a [bar chart](/docs/components/bar-chart/). For how many reach each stage of a process, use a [funnel](/docs/components/funnel/). For the parts of one whole, use a [meter](/docs/components/meter/) or a [donut chart](/docs/components/donut-chart/).

## Steps, subtotals and colours

The scale runs from zero, or from below it when the running total goes negative, to the highest point any bar reaches, and its axis writes large amounts short (`₹2.5L`), as a bar chart's does. A dashed line runs from each running total down to the step after it, which starts there.

`.colors(added, deducted, total)` sets the three colours: chart colours 1 and 2 for the steps and the text ink, `Ink::text()`, for the totals by default, so a total stands apart from the steps that make it. Use tones when adding means good and taking away bad.

```rust
use navree::ui::charts::*;
use navree::ui::*;

Waterfall::new("Studio profit, October")
    .locale("en-IN")
    .format(NumberFormat::currency("₹").max_decimals(0))
    .step("Classes", 4_20_000)
    .step("Retail", 85_000)
    .total("Revenue")
    .step("Instructors", -1_90_000)
    .step("Premises", -95_000)
    .step("Stock", -42_000)
    .total("Profit")
    .colors(Ink::tone(Tone::Success), Ink::tone(Tone::Danger), Ink::slot(7))
```

A step with no amount, `None` or a value that is not a finite number, is a row with no bar that leaves the running total as it was. A waterfall draws at most 24 rows, the first ones, and a note under it says so.

## Amounts and words

`.format(NumberFormat)` writes the amounts: `NumberFormat::currency("₹")` for money. `.locale("en-IN")` groups them in lakh and crore. The legend names the colours the rows use, in the order they meet them: "Total", "Added" and "Deducted". These words, the table's columns and the summary's template are fields of `WaterfallLabels`: change the ones you need and pass it to `.words(...)`.

```rust
use navree::ui::charts::*;

let mut words = WaterfallLabels::english();
words.added = "जोड़ा".into();
words.deducted = "घटाया".into();
Waterfall::new("भुगतान").locale("hi").words(words)
```

## Right to left and motion

`.rtl()` runs the bars from the right, for a right-to-left page. `.motion(Motion::new())` grows each bar from where it starts, one after another. The bars are drawn finished, so a reader who asks for less motion sees the final state.

## Loading, failed and empty

The states work as on a [line chart](/docs/components/line-chart/#loading-failed-and-empty). A waterfall with no amount is empty.

## The table

The table has a row for each row drawn: its label, its amount (with its sign for a step) and the running total after it. See [The table](/docs/components/line-chart/#the-table).

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `Tab` | Moves into the waterfall, which is one tab stop. |
| `↑`, `↓`, `←`, `→` | Moves to the row before or after. |
| `Home`, `End` | Moves to the first or the last row. |
| `Page Up`, `Page Down` | Moves a tenth of the way back or on, and at least one row. |
| `Esc` | Hides the tooltip. |

- The waterfall is a group named by its title and described by its summary: where it starts, where it ends, and how much was added and taken away on the way.
- A move by key marks the row and is announced politely: its label, its amount and the running total.
- Every row writes its amount in words beside its bar, and the legend names what each colour means, so the colour is never alone.
- With `.rtl()`, `←` and `→` swap.

</UiA11y>

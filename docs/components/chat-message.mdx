`ChatMessage` is one message of a [Conversation](/docs/components/conversation/). `ChatMessage::user` is what the reader wrote and `ChatMessage::assistant` is what the assistant wrote. Both take a key that names the message in the list, a cursor that places it, and the text.

```rust
use navree::ui::genui::*;

let labels = ChatLabels::default();
Conversation::new("kitchen-help", "/assistant/7/items").items([
    ChatMessage::user("m1", "0001", "Which city ordered the most this month?")
        .into_item(&labels),
    ChatMessage::assistant(
        "m2",
        "0002",
        "**Ludhiana** ordered the most: 1,240 orders worth ₹18,40,000.",
    )
    .into_item(&labels),
])
```

## What the words may be

What the reader wrote is plain text: its lines are kept and nothing in it is read as markup. What the assistant wrote is drawn with [Markdown](/docs/components/markdown/) from a source you do not control: no raw HTML, no pictures, links checked and marked with their host, headings starting at level 3, and at most 64 KB. A reply from a model can have lists, tables and code, and it cannot add an element, a script or an image that sends what the page knows to another site.

## States

An assistant's message has a state, set with `.state(..)`:

- `ChatState::Done` is the default.
- `ChatState::Writing` marks a message still being written. It shows a spinner and "Writing…", sets `aria-busy`, and is listed under its writing key (the key with `.w`, from `writing_key`), so it is never announced. `.stop(url, csrf)` adds a Stop button that posts to `url`.
- `ChatState::Stopped` shows a "Stopped" badge: the reader stopped the answer, and what was written stays.
- `ChatState::Failed` shows an alert, "The assistant couldn't answer. Try again."

<UiPreview example="chat-message/states" />

When the message is done it comes back under its own key, and the conversation announces it once.

## Who and when

A reader's messages lean to the end of the line and the assistant's to the start. The header says "You" or "Assistant", or the name you give with `.author(..)`. `.time(..)` adds a `<time>` you drew below the message.

## Reasoning and sources

`.with(..)` adds parts below the words: [Reasoning](/docs/components/tool-call-card/#reasoning), the sources of the answer ([Sources](/docs/components/sources/)), or both.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- A message is a plain element in the conversation's list. Who wrote it is in its header, in words.
- A finished assistant message is announced once, with "The assistant answered" and the start of its words. What the reader wrote is never announced back.
- The Stop button is a real form button, so it works with no script, and it is the only control a message draws.
- The side a message leans to is only visual. Inside `Direction::rtl`, start and end swap.

</UiA11y>

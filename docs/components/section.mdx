`Section` is a part of a page: an `id` that links name (`/#pricing`), a heading, its content, the page's width and gutters, and the space above and below. Its heading's title names the section for assistive technology.

```rust
use navree::ui::marketing::*;
use navree::ui::*;

let features = FeatureGrid::new()
    .feature(Feature::new("Sleep in stages", "Deep, REM and light sleep, night by night.").icon(icons::BED));

let section = Section::new("features")
    .heading(
        SectionHeading::new(("Five lenses on one body. ", accent("All from data you have.")))
            .eyebrow("What it reads")
            .lead("Every number says how it was worked out, from your own baseline.")
            .split(),
    )
    .child(features);
```

## Headings

`SectionHeading` has a small line above the title (`.eyebrow`), the title as an `h2`, a lead and an action. It sits at the start, `.center()`s, or `.split()`s the lead beside the title from 64 rem up, for a section whose content runs full width under it.

## Tone, width and space

`.tone(SectionTone::Muted)` raises the section's background one step, with hairlines above and below; cards inside it stand one step out of it. `.narrow()` keeps the content to the width of a column of text, for prose and questions. `.compact()` halves the space above and below, for a row of logos or a band of numbers. Two plain sections in a row are parted by a hairline.

`Hero` opens a page. Its headline is the page's one `h1`. With a picture it lays the words and the picture side by side from 64 rem up, and stacks them on a narrower screen; without one, or with `.centered()`, it centres the words. The picture can be a [Picture](/docs/components/picture/), a [BrowserFrame or PhoneFrame](/docs/components/device-frames/) around the live product, or any node.

```rust
use navree::ui::marketing::*;
use navree::ui::*;

let today = Picture::new("/img/today-1440.png", "Today in the Kesar app", 1440, 900);

let hero = Hero::new(("Know your body, ", accent("one calm score a day.")))
    .eyebrow("Health, read calmly")
    .lead("Kesar reads the sleep, heart and step data your phone already keeps.")
    .action(Button::primary("Start free").href("/signup").icon_end(icons::ARROW_RIGHT))
    .action(Button::outline("See how it works").href("/#how"))
    .note(icons::SHIELD_CHECK, "Data stored in India")
    .media(BrowserFrame::new(today).address("app.kesar.example/today"));
```

## Centred, and its backdrop

`.centered()` centres the words with the picture under them. `.backdrop(Backdrop::Glow)` (the default) draws soft light from the theme's accent behind the hero, `Backdrop::Grid` adds a fine grid fading from the middle, and `Backdrop::None` keeps the page's background. Each is drawn by the stylesheet from the theme's tokens and needs no image.

<UiPreview example="hero/centered" />

## The accent

`accent(text)` sets words of a heading in the accent colour: the half of a headline that carries it. A headline takes a tuple of text and nodes.

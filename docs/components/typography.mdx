`Heading` writes `<h1>` to `<h6>`; its look follows its level. `Text` writes a paragraph, or a `<span>` with `.inline()`. The body text is 14 px Geist; secondary text is 13 px.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Stack::gap(1).children((
    Heading::h3("Quarterly review"),
    Text::muted("Pipeline, wins and losses."),
))
```

## Headings

Pick the level from the page's outline, not from the size you want. In the [app shell](/docs/ui/app-shell/) the page title is the `<h1>`.

<UiPreview example="typography/headings" />

`Heading::h5` and `Heading::h6` are for the small headings inside a card or a panel. They link the parts stylesheet, which sizes them.

```rust
Heading::h5("Billing address")
```

## Text

<UiPreview example="typography/text" />

`Text::code` writes `<code>` in the system monospace font, which costs no download.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- Headings in order let screen reader users move through a page by its outline.
- Every text and background pair of the tokens reaches a 4.5:1 contrast; a test in the toolkit checks it.

</UiA11y>

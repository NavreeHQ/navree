`LegalPage` draws a long page of rules: its title (`h1`), an introduction and when it last changed, then its parts, each an `h2` that the contents link to. On a wide screen the contents sit beside the text and stay in view; on a phone they come first, in a box.

```rust
use navree::ui::marketing::*;
use navree::ui::*;

LegalPage::new("Privacy policy")
    .eyebrow("Legal")
    .intro("What Kesar collects, why, where it is kept and how to take it back.")
    .updated("Last updated 10 November 2026")
    .section(
        LegalSection::new("collect", "1. What we collect")
            .text("From your phone, with your permission: steps, sleep and heart rate.")
            .points(["Your name and email address, to sign you in."]),
    )
    .section(LegalSection::new("", "2. Where it is kept").text("On servers in Mumbai."))
```

## Parts

`LegalSection::new(id, title)` names a part; an empty id is made from the title. `.text` adds a paragraph, `.points` a list, and `.body` any content: a table, [Markdown](/docs/components/markdown/), a callout.

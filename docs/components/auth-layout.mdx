`AuthLayout` is the page of one form a guest fills in: signing in, signing up, choosing a new password. The form stands centred in the window, a panel beside it holds the app's mark, a line about it and a picture, and the top corner holds a theme menu. navree-auth's [`AuthPages`](/docs/basics/sessions-and-csrf/#the-pages) draws every page in it, in the app's own look.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;
use navree::ui::theme::{Appearance, Theme, ThemeMenu};

let card = Stack::gap(4).children((
    Heading::h1("Sign in"),
    Card::new().body(Text::new("The sign-in form goes here.")),
));
AuthLayout::new(card)
    .aside(Stack::gap(4).children((
        Heading::h2("Your health, clearly understood."),
        Text::new("Sleep, heart and steps, scored every day."),
    )))
    .aside_label("About Kesar")
    .corner(ThemeMenu::new(&Appearance::default(), &[Theme::NAVREE]).back("/sign-in"))
```

The panel shows from 960 px up. On a phone the form stands alone and first, so nothing pushes it below the fold.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- The form's part is the page's `main` landmark, with the id `main` a skip link points at.
- The panel is a `complementary` landmark named by `aside_label` ("About" by default).
- The corner comes before the form in the page, so Tab reaches the theme menu first; a form whose first field has `autofocus`, as the sign-in form does, starts there.

</UiA11y>

`Avatar` shows a person's picture with `Avatar::image(src, alt)`, their initials with `Avatar::initials(name)`, or a neutral person icon with `Avatar::placeholder(label)`.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Avatar::image("/avatars/gurpreet.webp", "Gurpreet Singh")
```

## When to use it

Use an avatar next to a person's name: in a list of members, a message, the user menu. Use the initials of an organisation, filled or in a tone, as its tile beside its name: in a workspace switcher.

## Initials

`Avatar::initials(name)` draws the first letter of the name's first word and of its last, in capitals: `DO` for "Dalbir Oberoi", `N` for "Narmada", `ਗਸ` for "ਗੁਰਪ੍ਰੀਤ ਸਿੰਘ". A screen reader says the whole name. A name with no letter or digit in it draws the person icon, as `placeholder` does.

`.tone(tone)` lays a thin tint of a [tone](/docs/components/badge/) behind the letters and draws them in the tone, as a badge in a tone is drawn. `.solid()` fills the avatar with the accent and draws the letters in the accent's ink, as a primary button is drawn: an organisation's tile. Both pairs are among the ones every theme is checked for, in light and in dark. A filled avatar takes no tone.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Avatar::initials("Narmada Roadways").solid();
Avatar::initials("Dalbir Oberoi").tone(Tone::Info).shape(AvatarShape::Circle);
```

<UiPreview example="avatar/initials" />

The initials, a tone, a fill and a circle are drawn by the parts stylesheet, which a page links only when it draws one of them.

## Shape

An avatar takes the theme's corner for avatars: 4 px, or what the theme's shape says with `Corners::avatar` (see [themes](/docs/ui/themes/)). `.shape(AvatarShape::Circle)` makes it round whatever the theme says, so a theme can give organisation tiles an 8 px corner and people round avatars.

## Sizes

Avatars take the controls' sizes. The initials are 12 px at the default size, 9 and 10 px at the two smaller and 15 px at the larger.

## When the picture fails

`.fallback()` shows the person icon when the picture does not load. It needs the parts script, so a page with one loads a small script; without it a picture that fails shows the browser's own broken-image mark.

```rust
Avatar::image("/avatars/gurpreet.webp", "Gurpreet Singh").fallback()
```

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- An image avatar's `alt` is required. Next to the person's name, pass the name anyway: it's what a screen reader says if the picture is the only thing in a link.
- A placeholder and an avatar of initials have `role="img"`, and a screen reader says the label or the whole name, never the letters.
- Images load lazily and carry their width and height, so the page doesn't shift.

</UiA11y>

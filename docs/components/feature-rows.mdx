`FeatureRows` tells features one after another. Each `FeatureRow` has a small line, a title (`h3`), paragraphs, points with ticks and an action, beside its picture. From 64 rem up the picture is on the right, then the left, in turn; `.media_first()` starts on the left. On a phone each picture is under its words.

```rust
use navree::ui::marketing::*;
use navree::ui::*;

FeatureRows::new()
    .row(
        FeatureRow::new("Last night, in stages")
            .eyebrow("Sleep")
            .text("Kesar splits the night into deep, REM and light sleep.")
            .point("Efficiency, debt and timing")
            .media(PhoneFrame::new(Picture::new("/img/sleep.png", "Last night: a sleep score of 84", 780, 1688))),
    )
```

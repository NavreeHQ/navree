`BrowserFrame` draws a browser window, with its buttons and an address, around what it holds; `PhoneFrame` draws a phone, its camera and its screen at a phone's shape. Each holds a [Picture](/docs/components/picture/) or the live product: charts, cards, any component.

```rust
use navree::ui::marketing::*;
use navree::ui::*;

let today = Picture::new("/img/today-1440.png", "Today in the Kesar app", 1440, 900);
let browser = BrowserFrame::new(today).address("app.kesar.example/today");

let sleep = Picture::new("/img/sleep-780.png", "Last night: a sleep score of 84", 780, 1688);
let phone = PhoneFrame::new(sleep);
```

## Light and dark

A frame follows the page's mode. `.mode(Mode::Dark)` or `.mode(Mode::Light)` fixes it, and what it holds is drawn in the same mode, components included: a dark screenshot stays dark on a light page.

<UiPreview example="device-frames/modes" />

## The camera and the status bar

The phone's camera stands over the top of its screen. A picture in a `PhoneFrame` starts below a strip the height of a phone's status bar, in the screen's colour, so the camera never covers the top of the app it shows. A screenshot taken on a phone brings a status bar of its own: `.edge_to_edge()` draws it from the very top.

## Captions

`.caption(text)` adds a line under the frame, saying what it shows.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- The frame is a `<figure>`; its bar and camera are hidden from assistive technology, and a caption is its `<figcaption>`.
- A picture inside needs its own alt text.

`FeatureGrid` lays features out in cards, in three columns by default from 64 rem up, two from 40 rem, one on a phone. Each `Feature` has an icon, a title (`h3`), a line or two, and optionally a link and a picture.

```rust
use navree::ui::marketing::*;
use navree::ui::*;

FeatureGrid::new()
    .cols(3)
    .feature(Feature::new("Sleep in stages", "Deep, REM and light sleep, night by night.").icon(icons::BED))
    .feature(Feature::new("Resting heart", "Against your own two weeks.").icon(icons::HEART))
    .feature(Feature::new("Private by design", "Stored in India, never sold.").icon(icons::LOCK).link("Read the privacy policy", "/privacy/"))
```

## A bento grid

`Feature::wide()` takes two columns from 64 rem up, and `.media(node)` puts a picture under the words: a chart, a screenshot, a part of the product. Mixed, they make a bento grid.

<UiPreview example="feature-grid/bento" />

## Without cards

`.plain()` lays the features out on the section's own background, with no card around each.

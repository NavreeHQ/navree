`Badge` is a short label: a deal's stage, an account's plan, a count. Its variant is a `BadgeVariant`, and a [DataTable](/docs/ui/data-table/) column draws one per cell with `#[col(badge = tone)]`.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Badge::new("Won").variant(BadgeVariant::Success)
```

## When to use it

Use a badge for a status or a category that a reader scans for. Green means good news, as it does everywhere in the toolkit.

## With an icon

`.icon(...)` puts an icon before the text and `.icon_end(...)` after it.

<UiPreview example="badge/icon" />

## Tones

A tone colours a badge by what it means. `Tone` has five built-in values (`Neutral`, `Info`, `Success`, `Warning`, `Danger`), and `Tone::named("violet")` picks one of the extra colours the stylesheet ships (`violet`, `cyan`, `rose`) or one your own stylesheet defines with `[data-tone=brand] { --tone: #7c5cff; }`. The five variants above stay as they are; a tone is the way to reach the other colours.

<UiPreview example="badge/tones" />

## A dot

`.dot()` adds a small round dot in the badge's colour before the text, for a status that should read at a glance in a column.

<UiPreview example="badge/dot" />

## Outcome chips

`Badge::outcome(label, reached)` is a chip for something that did or did not happen: paid, packed, shipped. When it did, it carries a check and its tone. When it did not, it carries a dash and a quiet, dashed outline. A screen reader hears "yes" or "no" after the label; change the words with `.outcome_words("oui", "non")`.

<UiPreview example="badge/outcome" />

Badges keep a 4 px corner. There is no pill shape.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- A badge is text in a `<span>`: it is read where it stands and isn't focusable.
- Colour doesn't carry the meaning alone: the text says the status. The icon and the dot are hidden from assistive technology.
- An outcome chip adds "yes" or "no" as text a screen reader hears. Sighted readers get the check or the dash and the dashed outline.

</UiA11y>

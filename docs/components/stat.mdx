`Stat` shows one figure, what it is and, with `.trend(text, good)`, how it's moving: green when that's good news, red when it isn't. `Bars` draws a small bar chart as SVG on the server, with no script and no `style` attribute.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Stat::new("Open deals", "312").trend("+12 this week", true)
```

## A chart under the figure

`Stat::chart(...)` puts a small chart under the figure, usually a [Sparkline](/docs/components/sparkline/) of its last periods. For the other charts, see the [line chart](/docs/components/line-chart/).

<UiPreview example="sparkline/default" />

## A change

`Stat::trend(text, good)` writes how a figure moved in your own words. `Stat::change(Trend)` writes it as a [Trend](/docs/components/trend/): an arrow, the change with its sign, and a color for what it means, with `up_is_bad()` for figures where a rise is bad news.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Stat::new("Resting heart rate", "58 bpm").change(Trend::new(3.0).up_is_bad())
```

## A tile for a dashboard

A dashboard puts more on each figure than a label and a number, and a `Stat` can carry it. Every piece is optional, and a stat that uses none is the plain one above.

```rust
use navree::ui::charts::Sparkline;
use navree::ui::*;

Stat::new("Resting heart rate", "58")
    .icon(icons::HEART_PULSE)
    .tone(Tone::Danger)
    .unit("bpm")
    .status(Badge::new("Athletic").tone(Tone::Success).dot())
    .change(Trend::new(-3.3).up_is_bad())
    .caption("vs last week")
    .chart(Sparkline::new("Resting heart rate, last 7 days", [61, 60, 62, 59, 58, 59, 58]).smooth().height(36))
```

<UiPreview example="stat/tile" />

- `icon(Icon)` puts an icon in a chip before the label, and `tone(Tone)` colours the chip by what the figure is about. The figure itself stays neutral: a colour on the number would say good or bad, which only a `status` or a `Trend` knows.
- `unit(...)` writes what the figure counts, smaller, after it: `bpm`, `%`, `h 20 min`.
- `status(...)` puts a pill, usually a [Badge](/docs/components/badge/), at the end of the label's line.
- `change(Trend)` and `caption(...)` write how it moved and against what: "+6.1% vs last week".
- `chart(...)` puts a [Sparkline](/docs/components/sparkline/) under the figure.
- `aside(...)` puts a small graphic beside it, at the end of the line: a [Ring](/docs/components/ring/) of 48 to 72 pixels toward a goal.
- `href(...)` makes the whole tile a link, with the label as its name.

<UiPreview example="stat/tile-ring" />

### A row of tiles

`Grid::fit(min)` is a grid of as many columns as fit, each at least `min` rem wide (8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20 or 24), so a row of tiles wraps by the room it has, from one column on a phone to six on a wide screen, with no breakpoint to choose.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Grid::fit(14).gap(4).children((
    Stat::new("Steps", "9,412").icon(icons::FOOTPRINTS).change(Trend::new(12.5)).caption("vs last week"),
    Stat::new("Sleep", "7").icon(icons::MOON).unit("h 20 min").change(Trend::new(6.1)),
    Stat::new("Distance", "6.8").icon(icons::MAP_PIN).unit("km"),
))
```

<UiPreview example="stat/tiles" />

The tile and the grid use theme tokens only, so they follow light and dark. A tile with an icon, a unit, a status, a caption, an aside or a link loads the app blocks stylesheet (about 2 KB gzip); a plain stat loads nothing more.

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- The trend's colour isn't its only sign: write the direction in the text, such as "+3.1%".
- The icon chip is decoration and is hidden from assistive technology: the label says what the tile is.
- A tile that is a link has one tab stop, on its label, and the whole tile is the target.
- A ring beside the figure is a picture named by its own title and described by its summary; the figure is the tile's text.
- A sparkline is a picture named by its title and described by its summary, with its values in a table that only a screen reader reads.
- `Bars` has `role="img"` and the description you give it, which is all assistive technology gets: say what the chart shows.

</UiA11y>

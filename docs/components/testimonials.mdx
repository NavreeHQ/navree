`Testimonials` lays quotes in columns that fill from the top, as many as the width allows. Each `Testimonial` has the quote (the card draws the quotation marks), who said it, who they are, their initials or photo, and stars.

```rust
use navree::ui::marketing::*;
use navree::ui::*;

Testimonials::new()
    .item(
        Testimonial::new("I finally know why Mondays feel heavy: Sunday nights.")
            .author("Simran Kaur Gill")
            .role("Teacher, Ludhiana")
            .initials("SG")
            .rating(5)
            .featured(),
    )
```

## Stars

`.rating(n)` draws `n` of five stars, and says the number in words for assistive technology: `5 out of 5`, or the words of `.rating_label(...)`.

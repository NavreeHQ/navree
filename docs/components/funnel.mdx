`Funnel` shows how many reach each stage of a process that has an order: viewed, added to the cart, started checkout, paid. Each stage is a bar as wide as its share of the first stage, with its count and that share beside it. From the third stage on, a line under the bar says what share of the stage before it reached this one.

```rust
use navree::ui::charts::*;
use navree::ui::*;

Funnel::new("Checkout funnel")
    .stage(Stage::new("Viewed a product", 9_412))
    .stage(Stage::new("Added to cart", 4_120))
    .stage(Stage::new("Started checkout", 2_204))
    .stage(Stage::new("Paid", 1_460).tone(Tone::Success))
```

It is an ordered list, so it reads in order without the picture. The stages step through one hue, faintest first, so the order is also in the color; the faintest still reaches 2:1 on the page or card under it. `Stage::tone(Tone)` colors a stage by what it means, such as the last as a success.

## When to use it

Use a funnel when each stage holds some of the one before: sign-up, activation, payment. If the stages are not nested, or can be reached in any order, use [bars](/docs/components/bar-chart/). For one rate, use a [Stat](/docs/components/stat/).

## Rates in your own words

A stage's share is worked out from the counts and written in the chart's number format and locale. When the server has already worded a rate, `Stage::rate(text)` shows it instead: "86% of signed up". The table keeps the worked-out shares.

<UiPreview example="funnel/rates" />

`.rtl()` makes the bars grow from the right, for a right-to-left page.

## Formats and languages

`.format(NumberFormat)` writes the counts, and the shares are percentages in the chart's locale. `.locale("de")` writes them as `43,8 %`. See [Formats and languages](/docs/components/line-chart/#formats-and-languages) on the line chart page for the formats, the locales and the words a chart writes. The columns of the table and the "Of previous" line are `ChartLabels` fields: `of_first` and `of_previous`.

## Loading, failed and empty

The states work as on a [line chart](/docs/components/line-chart/#loading-failed-and-empty), and keep the height of the stages. A funnel with no stage, or with no value in any stage, is empty.

<UiPreview example="funnel/states" />

## The table

The table has a row for each stage: its count, its share of the first stage and its share of the one before. `.table(TableMode)` says where it sits. See [The table](/docs/components/line-chart/#the-table).

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `Tab` | Moves into the chart, which is one tab stop. |
| `←`, `→`, `↑`, `↓` | Moves to the previous or the next stage. |
| `Home`, `End` | Moves to the first or the last stage. |
| `Page Up`, `Page Down` | Moves a tenth of the way back or on, and at least one stage. |
| `Esc` | Hides the tooltip. |

- The chart is a group named by its title and described by its summary: from the first stage and its count to the last and its count, and the share of the first that remains.
- Stages are an ordered list, so a screen reader reads their number and their order. Each says its name, its count and its share in text.
- A move by key marks the stage and is announced politely: its name, then its values.
- The table twin lists every stage. A stage with no value shows a dash that a screen reader reads as "No value".
- The steps of color are a second sign of order, never the only one: the order is the list's, and the share is in text. A tone colors a stage's bar, so keep what it means in the stage's name.

</UiA11y>

## Data rules

- A stage's value is a finite number. NaN and infinity are no value: the stage keeps its place and has no bar.
- Widths are shares of the first stage, from 0% to 100%. A stage larger than the first is drawn at 100%.
- A share of a stage with no value, or of a first stage at zero, is a dash.
- The stages keep the order you give them. Sort them in your query.
- A funnel draws at most 12 stages (`MAX_STAGES`), and says so under it when there were more. A process with more steps is a table.

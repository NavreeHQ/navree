`Pricing` lays plans side by side. When a plan has a monthly and a yearly price, a switch shows one or the other. The switch is a pair of radio buttons and the stylesheet shows the prices of the one chosen, so it works with no script, the arrow keys move between the two, and a hidden price is not read out. Write amounts as the reader reads them: `₹2,990`, `₹1,00,000`.

```rust
use navree::ui::marketing::*;
use navree::ui::*;

Pricing::new("plans")
    .yearly_badge("2 months free")
    .plan(Plan::new("Free").price("₹0", "for ever").action(Button::outline("Start free").href("/signup")))
    .plan(
        Plan::new("Plus")
            .monthly("₹299", "a month")
            .yearly("₹2,990", "a year")
            .yearly_note("₹249 a month, billed once a year")
            .feature("A year of trends")
            .action(Button::primary("Start Plus").href("/signup?plan=plus"))
            .featured("Most popular"),
    )
    .footnote("Prices in Indian rupees, including GST.")
```

## Yearly first

`.yearly()` checks the yearly choice when the page is drawn, for a link that carries `?billing=yearly`.

<UiPreview example="pricing/yearly" />

## A call to action for each period

`.yearly_action(button)` gives a plan a second call to action, shown while yearly is chosen, so each period links to its own checkout.

## Structured data

Give the plans to search engines as `Offer`s on your `SoftwareApplication`, with the amount in digits: `Offer::new("2990", "INR").name("Plus, yearly")`. See [Search, previews and answers](/docs/ui/seo/).

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

- The switch is a `<fieldset>` of two radio buttons named by a hidden legend (`.labels(...)` sets the words).
- The prices of the other period are `display: none`, so they are neither seen nor read.
- Each plan's name is an `h3`.

</UiA11y>

---
title: "Access control on routes"
description: "Deny by default: `#[public]`, `#[authenticated]`, `#[roles]`, `#[permissions]` and `#[can]`."
---

Every Navis route is denied unless it says who may call it. This page covers the access attributes `#[public]`, `#[authenticated]`, `#[roles]`, `#[permissions]` and `#[can]`, including loading the record a route acts on with `from = "id"`, and the errors callers see when access is refused.

`Sheet` is a [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog/) pinned to an edge: the right by default, or the left, top or bottom. It has everything a dialog has: the focus trap, `Esc`, focus return and no script.

The preview shows the sheet in place. Press **Filters** to open it for real.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Sheet::new("filters", "Filters")
    .side(SheetSide::Right)
    .body(Switch::new("hot").label("Hot deals only"))
    .footer(Button::primary("Apply"))
```

## When to use it

Use a sheet for a panel with more in it than a dialog holds: filters, a record's details, settings.

## Widths

A sheet on the left or right is 24 rem wide. `.width(SheetWidth::Lg)` makes it 40 rem, for a record's details: cards side by side, tabs, a timeline. `.width(SheetWidth::Xl)` makes it 60% of the screen and never less than `Lg`, for a record with a table in it, such as an order and its lines, while the page behind stays in view. On a screen narrower than a width plus 3 rem, the sheet is the screen less 3 rem.

```rust
let ticket = Sheet::new("ticket", "Ticket 2041")
    .width(SheetWidth::Lg)
    .body(details);

let order = Sheet::new("order", "Order 4821")
    .width(SheetWidth::Xl)
    .body(order_and_lines);
```

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `Tab`, `Shift+Tab` | Moves between the sheet's controls; focus stays inside. |
| `Esc` | Closes it. |

- The title names the sheet, as for a dialog.
- A side sheet is 24 rem wide, 40 rem with `SheetWidth::Lg`, or 60% of the screen with `SheetWidth::Xl`, and never more than the screen less 3 rem.

</UiA11y>

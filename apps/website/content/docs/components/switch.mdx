`Switch` is a checkbox drawn as a switch, with `role="switch"`. Assistive technology announces it as on or off, and it posts `name=true` with its form when it is on.

```rust
use navree::ui::*;

Switch::new("notify").label("Email me when a deal closes").checked(true)
```

## When to use it

Use a switch for a setting read as on or off. For agreeing to something, or picking items from a list, use a [Checkbox](/docs/components/checkbox/).

## A line under the label

`.description(text)` adds a line under the label that says more and ties it to the switch, so a screen reader reads it after the label.

```rust
Switch::new("notify")
    .label("Email me when a deal closes")
    .description("One email a day at most.")
```

## Keyboard and accessibility

<UiA11y>

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `Tab` | Moves focus to the switch. |
| `Space` | Turns it on or off. |

- `role="switch"` makes a screen reader say "on" and "off" rather than "checked".
- A switch posts with its form: it saves when the form is submitted.

</UiA11y>

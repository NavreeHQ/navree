---
title: "The crate hierarchy"
description: "The eight layers of Navree crates and the rules that keep them apart."
---

This page explains how Navree is split into small crates arranged in eight layers, from the kernel and macros at L0 to the facade and the CLI at L6. It covers the rule that a crate depends only on lower layers, how shared contracts in `navree-contracts` avoid sideways dependencies, and the test that enforces both.
